# PRICE ACTION PATTERNS & WYCKOFF PRINCIPLES FOR GOLD (XAUUSD)

## 1. High Probability Candlestick Formations
Candlestick signals must only be traded when they occur at high-timeframe Points of Interest (POI) such as Order Blocks or FVG boundaries.
- **Pin Bar (Rejection Wick)**: A candle with a wick that is at least 60% of the total candle range and a small real body near the opposite extreme. Signifies aggressive institutional absorption.
- **Engulfing Candle**: A candle whose real body completely covers the previous candle body. Requires above-average volume to confirm displacement.
- **Inside Bar Breakout**: Consolidation within the prior candle's high-low range, indicating compression before volatility expansion.

## 2. Wyckoff Accumulation and Distribution Schematics
- **Accumulation Phase**:
  - Preliminary Support (PS) followed by Selling Climax (SC).
  - Automatic Rally (AR) defines the range high; Secondary Test (ST) tests the low.
  - Phase C Spring: A sharp downward dip below the trading range that quickly reclaims the range. This is the optimal long entry point with tight risk.
  - Sign of Strength (SOS) and Last Point of Support (LPS): Higher lows forming before markup.
- **Distribution Phase**:
  - Buying Climax (BC) followed by Automatic Reaction (AR).
  - Upthrust After Distribution (UTAD): False breakout above the range high to trap breakout buyers before markdown.

## 3. Volume and Displacement Confirmation
- **Displacement**: Long-bodied candles closing near their extreme with minimal wicks, demonstrating institutional commitment.
- **Volume Climax vs Exhaustion**: Climax volume on ultra-wide spread candles at major resistance often indicates buying exhaustion, preparing for reversal.
- **Failed Breakouts (Fakeouts)**: When price breaks a key support/resistance level on declining volume and immediately closes back inside, initiate reversal trades targeting the opposite boundary.
